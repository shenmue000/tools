Small free tools

Each tool fixes one everyday data problem. Everything runs in your browser: your files are never uploaded.

  • Audacity point labels → region labels — turn markers into regions (pairs or marker-to-marker), preview, and download a label file to import back.
  • Track list → Audacity labels — paste YouTube timestamps, Discogs track lengths, a .cue sheet or just the names, and get labels to split an album or DJ set into named tracks.
  • Home Assistant: import historical energy data from CSV — turn a utility or meter CSV (15-min, 30-min, hourly or meter readings) into the hourly file the Import Statistics integration reads.
  • Observing list converter: SkySafari ↔ Stellarium ↔ CSV — move astronomy observing lists between SkySafari (.skylist), Stellarium and spreadsheets, including Telescopius exports.
  • Product CSV checker — find duplicate products, repeated SKUs and barcodes broken by Excel in a Shopify or WooCommerce export.
Made by a small team. Privacy: visits are counted anonymously (no cookies, no personal data); files you open stay on your computer.
